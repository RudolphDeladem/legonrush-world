## Registration

The user's satellite map (image 20, with IPS Road along its foot) is matched to the game map on two anchors:
- the round building with its diagonal wings (the Central Administration);
- the cross-shaped building labelled "UPSA Library".

The fit is x = 2361 + (px - 890) / 3.93, z = -971 + (py - 545) / 3.93. With it, the library's mapped outline lands within
2-4 m of the labelled building.

## What was built, and how sure it is

**Main Entrance** (`upsalandmarks.ts` portal; `upsalandmarksplan.ts` PORTAL). The older blue-and-white canopy gate, its
gatehouses and its fascia banner are removed. `upsacampus.ts` keeps only the campus wall.

The new portal has:
- white square tiles (0.3 m) on every face;
- the quarter arch rising from the ground at the outline's west edge to the header, its band 2.3 m wide at the foot;
- the deep soffit at 7.2 m with eight skylights;
- the header's top at 10.2 m;
- the tall tiled block on the east, with blue windows on both fronts and toward the road, and the guard post's door;
- on the outer fascia: the name, the motto "Scholarship with Professionalism", "UPSA", and the crest at the arch's top;
- the booth under the arch;
- the two lift barriers, raised;
- the ramp with black rails, and the planting bed with its curved kerb at the arch's foot;
- grey paving in front and through the passage, either side of the road.

Confidence and limits:
- **Opening.** The road's opening (x 2285.2-2297.2) stays open its whole height. The arch is 5.7 m above the opening's
  west edge.
- **Heights.** These are read from the photographs: estimates.
- **Lane count and barriers.** The photos do not settle them, so the earlier two raised barriers are kept.
- **North front.** The campus side's lettering is not shown in any photo, so it is left plain. Its arch is the outer
  arch mirrored.
- **Crest.** It is drawn plainly, not copied.

**KOKA.**
- **Massing.** The two tall white tiled side masses (23.5 m) carry vertical slot windows. The hall between them has a
  blue metal roof, colour from the aerial (image 32).
- **Glass front.** The broad convex blue glass curtain wall is an arc of radius 51.7 m through the outline's bulge, from
  8.4 m to 20.2 m. It sits over a navy floor band, above the dark-column entrance recessed beneath it, with three steps.
- **Forecourt.** The paved forecourt runs to the walk, with lamps and trees.
- **Not modelled.** No KOKA name sign was added, because no photo shows one. The rear and the fly tower are not shown in
  the photos, so they are plain. Its capacity was not used to size it.

**The court.**
- **Fountain.** It sits in the diamond where the mapped walks meet, at the middle of the quadrangle, as images 11, 34 and
  the pack's aerial show. It has a stepped orange-brown rim, a white pebble ring, and jets.
- **Benches.** Eight grey benches stand beside the walks, 2.7 m off their centrelines.
- **Letters.** The "We love UPSA" letters stand on the lawn toward KOKA (image 29).
- **Spots.** The spots of the benches, lamps and letters are inferred.

**Justice Aryeetey Building.**
- **Massing.** Six storeys (3.4 m) on the mapped plan round its two courtyards, with a wide blue-glazed window to each
  bay and white slab bands.
- **West front.** Six broad blue strips run up it, with two on each end. The central blue circulation bay has dark
  stair glazing and an arched cap, at the mapped drive above the existing entrance canopy (`upsacampus.ts` core). The
  game ride finishes there.
- **Strip positions.** These are inferred from images 9 and 11.

**Opoku Ampomah Building / Central Administration.**
- **Drum.**
  - The lower floors stand on the whole outline. The drum without its links rises to 14.4 m.
  - The curved blue glass band (8.2-13.8 m) leans out round the west front between the links.
  - Under the band is the white OPOKU AMPOMAH BUILDING band.
  - Over the entrance glazing is the grey CENTRAL ADMINISTRATION band.
  - "UPSA" and the crest stand on the roof edge, and a flagpole stands by the entrance.
- **Wings.** Three storeys with blue cladding panels round white-framed windows, under pale blue hipped roofs (images 19,
  23).
- **Heights.** Estimates.

**Joshua Alabi Library.** (That spelling throughout; "Larbi" is never used.)
- **Massing.** Six storeys on the mapped plan. Its ranges and arms carry blue windows, white floor bands and the small
  breeze-block squares.
- **Central bay.** The central north arm is the taller entrance bay: a seventh storey with the arched window and a
  pediment. It has blue columns in pairs either side of the glazed doors, the JOSHUA ALABI LIBRARY sign above them, and
  shallow steps.
- **Front.** The north face is taken as the front. It faces the mapped road and is the only face whose middle projects,
  symmetrical with wings either side. Medium confidence: the mapped arms project further than the photos suggest, and
  the map is followed.

## Status classes

**(a) Confirmed by user images:**
- the new gate's design;
- KOKA's curved blue glass and the fountain court;
- the Administration's two sign bands;
- the library's arched central bay;
- the towers' white and blue facades.

**(b) Confirmed by UPSA sources (as cited in the brief):**
- the names;
- KOKA's capacity and dates;
- Justice Aryeetey next to the auditorium;
- the towers' ten storeys.

**(c) Inferred, validated against the map:** every footprint and heading above.

**(d) Unknown:**
- the gate's lane count;
- the campus-side lettering;
- KOKA's rear;
- the library's exact front;
- any sign text the photos do not show.

## Checks

`npm run test:routes` has a section for this pack. It checks that:
- each landmark's outline is built by its model;
- the portal keeps the gate's opening and its headroom;
- no solid of these models stands on a road or a walk;
- the ride from the UPSA Hostel to UPSA passes none of them;
- the library is named the Joshua Alabi Library.

The UPSA section's own checks were updated:
- "the campus wall";
- the towers' outline points on osm:way/885510985.
