# Campus reference audit: "Building views from the game vs street view in reality" (38 pages)

The owner rode the game to fixed positions, took screenshots, and paired each with a photograph of the same place.
This audit covers every page. Each page lists: what the images are; where the place is in the game; the viewing
direction; what differs; the correction; and what is uncertain.

**Images.** The PDF's images are saved as `docs/handover/images/294.jpg`–`362.jpg`, in the PDF's order (`img`
numbers below are the PDF's embedded image index):
- image *i* is saved as `294 + i` for i ≤ 60;
- image 61 is a 2×2 px blank, so it was dropped;
- image *i* is saved as `293 + i` for i ≥ 62.

**Telling game from photo.** Game screenshots carry the game's interface:
- the "End tour" pill;
- the "Steer ← → (A / D) …" hint;
- the route bar (most read "TO NBLOCK 0.57 km");
- the minimap;
- the rider.

Photographs have none of these. Captions are printed under, over or beside the images; the order varies from page to
page, so each pairing below was checked against what the images show.

## Baseline: how the game builds the campus (what can be corrected in place)

**Engine and entry points**
- The game is Vite + TypeScript + Three.js.
- Entry points: `src/main.ts` → `src/game/Game.ts`.
- The campus is built in `buildCampus()` (`src/game/campus.ts`), which assembles the layers below.

**Map data**
- `src/data/legon-map.json` is generated by `npm run geo` from OSM, Google Open Buildings and the owner's
  registry.
- The registry is `data/geography/registry/corrections.json` (exclude, heights, reshape, addAreas, addWays) and
  `access.json`.
- It holds the road graph, building footprints with heights, areas, places and access points.

**Coordinates**
- Game metres: x = (lng + 0.1871)·110778.848 (east), z = (5.6518 − lat)·110574 (south). North is −z.

**Buildings**
- Every mapped footprint is extruded by the generic builder (`src/game/campus.ts` / `buildings.ts`). That is where
  the plain cream two-storey blocks with red hipped roofs in the screenshots come from.
- Buildings the owner has documented are modelled as **sites** with the block engine `src/game/blocks.ts`
  (`createSite(name, specs)`). The sites are listed in `src/game/sites.ts`.
- A site replaces the generic footprint (`replaces`), keeps props out (`keep`), and adds its own geometry
  (`extras`).

**Ground**
- `src/game/relief.ts` holds the zones (terraces, hollows, a hill) and the stairways.
- Everything that stands on the ground asks `groundHeight(x, z)`.

**Collision and routing**
- `src/game/solids.ts` holds the SOLIDS grid and PASSAGES for the free-ridden bike.
- Routes run on the road graph and are tested by `npm run test:routes`.

**Checks**
- `npx tsc --noEmit -p .`
- `npm run geo`
- `npm run test:routes`
- `npm run build`
- `npm run smoke`
- `.preview/lens.mjs` (route lengths)
- `.preview/camcheck.mjs` (cameras outside buildings)

Every structure in this PDF is one of three kinds:
- a **generic footprint**: Graduate School, Doctorate, the construction-site blocks, the Research and Innovation
  Complex, the two-storey block before Volta Hall Annex, the mast compound, the Chemistry Department, the uncompleted
  building's car park;
- an existing **site** whose model differs from the photos: Nursing (`labs.ts`), Volta Hall Annex (`volta.ts`), the
  Chemistry Department Extension and the tall range north of Physics (`physics.ts`), the Balme Library's east side
  (`balme.ts`);
- **missing**: the construction hoardings, the security post, the fences.

### Pages 1–4: School of Nursing and Midwifery (views 1–4)

**Images**
- Game screenshots: img 0, 2, 4, 6.
- Photographs: img 1, 3, 5, 7.

**Place**
- OSM footprint "School Of Nursing and Midwifery": x −355…−308, z −199…−181. Modelled in `labs.ts`.
- The game views look along the building from the grass east and south of it. On their right are the
  three-storey red-roofed white block (footprint x −303…−277, z −267…−224) and the Larway Oraca Building.

**What the photos show**
- **Massing and floors.**
  - A white, flat-roofed, three-storey concrete building of the 1970s.
  - The ground floor is set back on round columns; the soffits are lit, and the walls behind are white with grilles.
  - The two floors above are solid white walls with few windows: small louvre windows and one wider window.
  - The upper floors overhang the ground floor. The top floor cantilevers furthest at the near end, which gives a
    stepped, inverted profile.
  - A deep fascia runs round the top, with the sign SCHOOL OF NURSING AND MIDWIFERY / COLLEGE OF HEALTH SCIENCES /
    UNIVERSITY OF GHANA / LEGON.
- **The composition along the facade**, nearest first:
  1. the main block with the sign;
  2. an open-gallery bay: corridors behind parapets, recessed;
  3. the entrance core: stacked small balconies with rails, a second sign over the lit entrance;
  4. a tall blank stair tower, slightly taller than the rest;
  5. a three-storey rear block with small windows and air-conditioners;
  6. a lean-to shed with blue doors at its foot.
- **Around it.**
  - A large asphalt car park with white bay lines runs along the front, edged by kerbs and hedges.
  - There are palms (a coconut palm and a fan palm), a curved paved walk and flower beds.
  - At the end of the car park is a gate in a fence of grey corrugated sheets and a white palisade, with a big
    spreading tree beyond.
  - Across the car park stands the three-storey white building with a red roof and green-tinted windows.

**What the game shows**
- The current model was built from an earlier photo of the other face. It has continuous balconies with slotted
  parapets, yellow-and-dark louvre bays, grilles across the whole ground floor, and a lawn instead of the car park.

**Correction**
- Rebuild the front as the photos show: the main block with the stepped overhangs and the sign, the gallery bay, the
  entrance core with stacked balconies, the tall stair tower, the rear block and the shed.
- Give it the asphalt car park along the front, with bay lines and kerbs.
- Add the corrugated-sheet fence and gate at the end, and the palms and hedges.
- Keep the footprint, which agrees with the photos' proportions.

**Uncertain**
- Which long side is the front. The car park, the fence line and the red-roofed block across it fit the **south**
  side (toward J.K.M. Hodasi Road), but this is not proven.
- The back (unseen) is kept plain.
- Floor heights are estimated at 3.3 m from the window and person scale.
