# LEGONRUSH

Before working on this repository, read the handover documents in this order:

1. `docs/migration/NEW_ACCOUNT_START_HERE.md`: entry point, orientation and the owner's rules.
2. `docs/migration/PROJECT_MASTER_HANDOVER.md`: identity, state, history, architecture, how the owner works.
3. `docs/handover/HANDOVER.md` and `docs/handover/TASKS.md`: the first account's handover and every completed batch
   (147 so far).
4. `docs/LEGON_BUILDINGS.md`: building details and the owner's decisions per batch (read the latest sections first).

Then, as the task needs:

- `docs/migration/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS_AND_DECISIONS.md`: requirements and standing owner decisions.
- `docs/migration/GIT_AND_DEPLOYMENT_RUNBOOK.md`: branches, checks before every push, publishing, rollback.
- `docs/migration/OPEN_ISSUES_AND_NEXT_ACTIONS.md`: open questions and known issues.
- `docs/migration/FEATURE_AND_ROADMAP_STATUS.md`, `GEOGRAPHY_AND_CAMPUS_FIDELITY.md`,
  `VISUAL_DESIGN_AND_ASSET_SPECIFICATION.md`, `CONVERSATION_CONTEXT_AND_DECISION_LOG.md`, `ASSET_INVENTORY.md`.

The owner's pictures are in `docs/handover/images/` (indexed in `docs/handover/IMAGE_INVENTORY.md`) and the briefs,
packs and prompt files they attached are in `docs/handover/uploads/`. The conversation is in
`docs/handover/conversation-log.md` (2026-10-06 → 10-09) and `conversation-log-2.md` (2026-10-09 → 10-13). Each
recent brief has a folder under `docs/` (for example `docs/okponglo/`, `docs/great-hall/`) with its evidence audit and
open questions. Copy `tools/preview/*` to `.preview/` for the
preview camera views and the route-length and camera checks.
